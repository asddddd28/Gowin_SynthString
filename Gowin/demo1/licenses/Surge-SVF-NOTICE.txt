This project's fixed-point transition and RTL pipeline are adaptations of
the standard SVFLP12Aquad, SVFHP12Aquad and SVFBP12Aquad algorithms. The
upstream headers remain unchanged. The FPGA kernel is not the full Surge
synthesizer, nor a port of its Driven/Clean or 24dB variants.

Adaptation author: Codex, 2026-10-12, for this project.
The standalone synthesis requires constraints/surge_svf_sample.gsc.
The voice state and shared return pack are independently verified modules;
their 32-slot feedback connection is currently a simulation harness, not
an integrated board audio path or a hardware-tested Surge patch engine.

2026-10-13 continuation support (GPL-3.0-only within this adaptation):
  model/surge_wavetable_schedule.py

These support compact interpolation and an independent phase schedule.
The shared-filter arbitration and VCA connection remain a simulation prototype;
no complete Surge preset engine or new board release is claimed.

2026-10-13 public-port wave read continuation (GPL-3.0-only):
  model/wavetable_pair_read.py
  scripts/validate_wavetable_pair_read.py
  scripts/record_wavetable_pair_read_results.py
  rtl/synth/WavetableMorphFused.v
  rtl/synth/WavetablePairRead.v
Five-edge interpolation preserves the existing fixed-point floor operations.
The read data path accepts external synchronous sine and user-table pairs;
legacy writable cache, behaviorally modeled sine ROM, arbitration and tail
verification do not constitute a new board-integrated Surge preset engine.

2026-10-13 frame lifecycle and atomic voice-ingress continuation
(GPL-3.0-only within this adaptation):
  model/surge_frame_control.py
  scripts/validate_surge_frame_control.py
  scripts/record_surge_frame_control_results.py
  rtl/synth/WavetableFrameGuard.v
  rtl/synth/SurgeVoiceIngress.v
The frame guard and two mailboxes are separately synthesized control modules.
Their simulated connection verifies whole-frame cancellation and reuse through
the standard SVF owner boundary. The writable cache, sine ROM hardware,
native VCA/master/I2S path and complete board integration remain unfinished.

2026-10-13 captured amplitude and shared voice-tail continuation
(GPL-3.0-only within this adaptation):
  model/surge_voice_tail.py
  scripts/validate_surge_voice_tail.py
  scripts/record_surge_voice_tail_results.py
  rtl/synth/SurgeVoiceTailTag.v
  rtl/synth/SurgeVoiceVca.v
  rtl/synth/SurgeFrameAccumulator.v
The VCA preserves the previous fixed-point amplitude floor operations and
consumes parameters captured with the accepted filter request. The accumulator
produces an exact signed64 sum of32 unique qualified voices in one frame.
These three leaf modules are individually synthesized and connected to a
verified shared-filter simulation fixture. Native amplitude-envelope generation,
master gain, clipping,24-bit I2S and board integration remain unfinished.

2026-10-13 full-width fixed-point PCM boundary continuation (GPL-3.0-only):
  model/surge_pcm_output.py
  scripts/validate_surge_pcm_output.py
  scripts/record_surge_pcm_output_results.py
  rtl/audio/SurgePcmOutput.v
This module captures a nonnegative Q16 gain per frame, preserves the full
signed64 mix product, rounds symmetrically and saturates the final24-bit PCM.
The frozen public I2sTx/PcmRounder modules are used in simulation to verify
24-bit I2S and16-bit PT8211 transmission. Native Surge dB conversion, block
smoothing, master mute and selectable float clipping are not yet reproduced.
Complete board integration and physical audio verification remain unfinished.
